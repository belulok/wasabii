Wasabi

Sharp execution for OpenSea SeaDrop mints

Paste a collection. Wasabi reads the drop straight off the contract — supply, stages, per-wallet caps, and whether your wallet actually passes — then signs offline and submits direct to the sequencer, skipping the public gateway that costs most people half a second.
